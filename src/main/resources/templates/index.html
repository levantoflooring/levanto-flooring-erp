The Levanto Standard

Architectural Protocol

Every project adheres to a four-stage engineering methodology ensuring moisture equilibrium and surface longevity.

01

Moisture & Base Audit

Electronic calcium chloride testing and subfloor relative humidity mapping before any material delivery.

02

Substrate Leveling

Laser-measured diamond grinding and self-leveling cementitious screed to achieve sub-millimeter flatness.

03

Precision Laying

Master artisan alignment, perimeter expansion calibration, and acoustic underlayment bonding.

04

Quality Certification

Comprehensive finishing audit, protective curing cover, and written warranty documentation handover.
