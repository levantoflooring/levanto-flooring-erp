The Levanto Standard

Architectural Protocol

Every project adheres to a four-stage engineering methodology ensuring moisture equilibrium and surface longevity.

01

Moisture & Base Audit

Electronic calcium chloride testing and subfloor relative humidity mapping before any material delivery.

02

Substrate Leveling

Laser-measured diamond grinding and self-leveling cementitious screed to achieve sub-millimeter flatness.

03

Precision Laying

Master artisan alignment, perimeter expansion calibration, and acoustic underlayment bonding.

04

Quality Certification

Comprehensive finishing audit, protective curing cover, and written warranty documentation handover.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Answers to the questions we hear most from homeowners, architects, and project directors.

How long does a typical hardwood floor installation take? ▾
For a standard 1,500–2,000 sq ft residence, hardwood installation typically takes 4–7 working days, including our mandatory moisture and subfloor audit, diamond grinding, and two-stage finishing. Larger projects or intricate parquet patterns such as herringbone or chevron may require 10–14 days.
Do you provide a warranty on waterproofing work? ▾
Yes. Our standard waterproofing systems carry a 10-year anti-leak performance warranty. Premium crystalline barrier systems and polyurethane liquid membranes are covered by written documentation that includes direct technical accountability — not through a third-party insurer.
Can epoxy flooring be installed over existing tiles or concrete? ▾
In most cases, yes. Our team performs a substrate assessment to check for tile adhesion, moisture levels, and surface flatness. Existing tiles may need to be removed if there is moisture ingress or de-bonding. Concrete surfaces require diamond grinding to achieve the sub-millimeter profile needed for a flawless epoxy bond.
What is the minimum area for a site visit / consultation? ▾
We welcome all project sizes. There is no minimum sq ft requirement for a site visit. We service single-room renovations, full-villa flooring, and large-scale commercial installations. Contact us via the form or WhatsApp and our team will schedule an assessment at no charge.
Is SPC / Luxury Vinyl suitable for Kerala's humid climate? ▾
Absolutely — SPC (Stone Plastic Composite) is one of our top recommendations for Kerala specifically because of its 100% waterproof rigid core. Unlike traditional hardwood, SPC will not expand, warp, or cup in high-humidity environments. The IXPE foam base provides additional thermal comfort and sound dampening ideal for concrete slab construction common in Kerala homes.
Do you handle both pre-construction and post-construction termite treatment? ▾
Yes, we provide both. Pre-construction treatment involves chemical barrier application to soil and foundation during the slab stage — far more effective and economical. Post-construction treatment uses drilling, injection, and reticulation piping systems to create barriers around existing structures. Both are IS:6313 compliant.
How do I maintain my epoxy or hardwood floor after installation? ▾
Epoxy floors require only damp mopping with a neutral pH cleaner — avoid acid or bleach-based products. Hardwood floors finished with UV-cured hardwax oil should be cleaned with a recommended wood soap and re-oiled every 2–4 years depending on traffic. We provide a complete care kit and printed maintenance guide with every project handover.
